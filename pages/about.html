About Us

A Small Team Doing Big Things

IVPRO Tech Solutions was founded by a team of experienced engineers with expertise spanning industrial automation, artificial intelligence, machine vision, enterprise software, cloud technologies, and smart manufacturing solutions.

Having delivered complex engineering and software solutions for global customers across Germany, the United Kingdom, the United States, and leading OEM manufacturers, our founders recognized the growing need for intelligent, integrated solutions within the manufacturing sector.

At IVPRO, we bridge the gap between industrial automation and advanced software technologies by combining PLC automation, machine vision, artificial intelligence, Industrial IoT, cloud platforms, and enterprise applications into practical, scalable solutions that deliver measurable business value.

Our mission is simple: Transform industrial challenges into intelligent solutions.
