The Challenge

Human Errors Are Inevitable — Unless Prevented

In complex assembly operations, even well-trained operators make mistakes. Wrong parts, missing steps, incorrect sequences — each error leads to rework, waste, and customer complaints.

Poka-Yoke systems make errors impossible at the source, ensuring every product is built right the first time.

Assembly Validation

Step-by-step verification ensures each Assembly stage is completed correctly.

Mistake-Proof Workflows

Guided workflows that enforce correct sequences and prevent step skipping.

Vision-Based Verification

Camera systems that verify correct part placement and assembly completeness.

Barcode Verification

Scan and verify parts against BOM to ensure correct components are used.

Operator Guidance

Screen-based work instructions with visual guides for each assembly step.
